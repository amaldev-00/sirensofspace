# sirensofspace
Scroll through time (1967 to 2024) and hear 15 retired NASA machines tell their own stories.

Challenge: NASA Space Apps 2026, telling the story of hardware left on the Moon, Mars and in deep space.
Team: AXD (AMAL DEV S S)

Facts from NASA's mission pages and the NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov).
The machines' voices are imagined; dates and numbers are real.

Use of AI: the code and story text were written with help from Claude (an AI assistant). The idea and creative direction are the creator's own.
Not made by or endorsed by NASA.
