Left Behind

Fifteen NASA machines. They finished their jobs. Now they tell you their stories.

Where the facts come from

The machines’ voices are imagined to make the stories fun. The dates and numbers come from NASA’s mission pages:

  • Apollo Lunar Module (NSSDCA)
  • Lunar Roving Vehicles (NSSDCA)
  • ALSEP science packages (NSSDCA)
  • Surveyor 3
  • Mars Pathfinder and Sojourner
  • Spirit and Opportunity
  • Viking 1
  • InSight
  • Dawn
  • NEAR Shoemaker
  • Pioneer 10
  • Ingenuity Mars Helicopter
  • Kepler Space Telescope
  • NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive
Use of AI: the code and story text were written with help from Claude, an AI assistant, using the NASA pages above. The idea and creative direction came from the project’s creator. The art is drawn with simple shapes, with no NASA logos or images. This project is not made by or endorsed by NASA.
